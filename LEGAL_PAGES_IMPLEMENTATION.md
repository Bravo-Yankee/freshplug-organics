# Legal Pages Implementation Guide

## 📋 **Overview**

Created comprehensive Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages specifically tailored for Freshplug Organics Poultry Farm's operations in Kenya. These documents cover all aspects of the agricultural e-commerce business.

## 📄 **Pages Created**

### **1. Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`)**
- **Comprehensive data protection coverage**
- **Kenya-specific legal compliance** (Data Protection Act, 2019)
- **Agricultural business considerations**
- **E-commerce and food safety aspects**

### **2. Terms of Service (`terms-of-service.html`)**
- **Complete business terms and conditions**
- **Poultry-specific regulations and requirements**
- **Kenya Consumer Protection Act compliance**
- **Live animal sales and food safety coverage**
